About Us

Our History

Natal International High School is located in Mpigi District, Muduuma Sub-county, just a few kilometers from the main road. Our school is privately owned and was established in 2012 by Mr. Mukasa Bernard Natal.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide affordable, quality education to the people of our community and all of Uganda. We believe that every child and citizen is entitled to education at an affordable price, regardless of race, background, religion, or tribe.

Our Vision

To produce well-equipped, God-fearing students with the knowledge, skills, and values necessary for success in life.

Our Motto

"In Prayers We Trust"

Religious Foundation

Natal International High School is a Christian-based institution. However, we warmly welcome students of all faiths and beliefs, fostering an inclusive and respectful environment for all.

Core Values

  • Honesty
  • Assertiveness
