Our Services

Educational Services

We provide comprehensive education to students from Senior One through Senior Six. Our school employs trained teaching and non-teaching staff who are dedicated to supporting student learning.

We maintain a fully-equipped library stocked with diverse learning resources to aid students in their academic pursuits and help them achieve excellence.

Skills Training Programs

The school partners with Sawa World Uganda to offer comprehensive skills training sessions. During these programs, students acquire practical skills including:

  • Vocational and technical competencies
  • Entrepreneurship and business skills
  • Digital literacy
  • Life skills and personal development

Students apply these skills in their communities during school holidays, fostering creativity, innovation, and self-sustainability in today's modern world.

Co-Curricular Activities

Students participate in diverse co-curricular activities that complement their academic learning, including:

  • Sports: Football, Netball, Volleyball, Basketball
  • Cultural activities and performances
  • Club memberships

These activities refresh students' minds, promote teamwork, and develop leadership qualities.

Academic Excellence

We have consistently performed well over the past years, emerging as the best in our district for UCE (Ordinary Level) and UACE (Advanced Level) examinations.

Our commitment to academic excellence is evident in our student success rates.
